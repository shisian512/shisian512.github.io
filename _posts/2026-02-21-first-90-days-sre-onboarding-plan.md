> Summary: This roadmap outlines how a new SRE can build confidence and deliver value in the first 90 days without trying to learn everything at once.

A common onboarding trap is attempting deep ownership too early. A better approach is staged learning: understand the system, support operations safely, then drive targeted improvements.

---

## Days 1-30: Build a System Map

Primary goals:

- Understand core services and user journeys.
- Learn deployment flow and environment structure.
- Review existing dashboards, alerts, and runbooks.
- Shadow incident response and release processes.

Expected outcomes:

- A clear architecture map.
- A list of critical dependencies.
- A baseline understanding of reliability expectations.

---

## Days 31-60: Contribute Safely

Primary goals:

- Handle low-risk operational tasks with supervision.
- Improve one runbook or alert rule.
- Participate in postmortems and action tracking.
- Start measuring one reliability pain point.

Expected outcomes:

- First operational improvements merged.
- Better familiarity with production tooling.
- Stronger confidence in on-call procedures.

---

## Days 61-90: Own a Reliability Improvement

Primary goals:

- Lead one scoped reliability initiative.
- Define measurable success criteria.
- Coordinate with development and platform teams.
- Report outcomes and next-step recommendations.

Expected outcomes:

- Delivered improvement with measurable impact.
- Clear operational ownership in at least one area.
- A prioritized reliability backlog for future iterations.

---

## Suggested Weekly Cadence

- 1 architecture or systems deep-dive session.
- 1 runbook or incident review session.
- 1 improvement task tied to a measurable reliability outcome.

This rhythm keeps learning practical and connected to production needs.

---

## Final Thoughts

A strong first 90 days is not about speed alone. It is about building trust, operational judgment, and repeatable engineering habits.

For new SREs, consistency and clarity are usually more valuable than early heroics.
